\chapter*{Abstract}

This thesis aims to point out the possibilities of current deep learning text information extraction systems that process German language texts.
In recent years, advances in the field of natural language processing have made tremendous progress. 
Similar to the advances in the field of computer vision, modern language models nowadays are able to solve problems which would have been unthinkable ten years ago.
This paper demonstrates the current possibilities to extract information from text data that have emerged over recent years.

Of particular interest is the pipeline that receives unstructured text as input, transforms the unstructured data into a machine-readable format, and then applies various techniques to extract useful information such as dates or named entities. Furthermore, to make sense out of the extracted information, these recognized entities have to be further processed, in order to make sense of them, e.g. disambiguation and linking. It takes into account the peculiarities of the needed German-language models as well as specific problems one encounters with German written text.

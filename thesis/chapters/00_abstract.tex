\chapter*{Abstract}
This thesis implements Named Entity Recognition (\acro{NER}) in German-language text data dissemninated via the messenger service Telegram, with a focus on the mobilization for protests against measures to counteract the COVID-19 in Germany. 

This work explores the potentials and limitations of two existing German-language models for NER, namely \textit{spacy} and \textit{hugging face}, for the recognition of the entity types \textsc{PERSON}, \textsc{LOCATION}, \textsc{ORGANIZATION}, \textsc{DATE} and \textsc{TIME} for this specific domain and data. Furthermore, the recognition of an additional custom entity type \textsc{ACTION} will be explored. 

Additionally, I will explore the potentials of Machine Translation (MT) for the improvement of German-language NER models.

This thesis contributes to the task of German-language NER in general by applying it to a specific domain and data type, namely text data from messenger services. Furthermore, its aim is to provide a low-resource tool for both researchers and civil society for monitoring and analyzing large amount of text data from social media/messengers.
